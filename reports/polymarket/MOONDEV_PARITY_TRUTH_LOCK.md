# MoonDev Parity Truth Lock

This build separates exploration learning from promotion-quality trades.

## Rules

- No fake candles
- No fake order book
- No fake win-rate
- No fake 95%
- Unknown/empty events are quarantined
- Exploration events train Nexora
- Promotion events must be MoonDev-ranked and risk-approved
- 95% only passes with real counted paper evidence

## Routes

- /api/nexora/moondev-parity/status
- /api/nexora/moondev-parity/promotion

## Safety

- Live trading enabled: NO
- Private keys inside Nexora: NO
- Wallet signing inside Nexora: NO
- Bank transfers: NO
