# Nexora Paper Practice Supervisor

Adds read-only supervisor routes for the paper practice loop.

## Routes

- /api/nexora/paper-practice/status
- /api/nexora/paper-practice/log
- /api/nexora/paper-practice/runs
- /api/nexora/paper-practice/commands

## Safety

- Live trading enabled: NO
- Live orders enabled: NO
- Private keys inside Nexora: NO
- Wallet signing inside Nexora: NO
- Autonomous money movement: NO
