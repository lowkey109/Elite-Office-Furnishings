Step 2: patch every risky line

Change patterns like this:

data.slice(0, 6)

to:

(data ?? []).slice(0, 6)

and this:

data.items.slice(0, 6)

to:

(data?.items ?? []).slice(0, 6)

and for strings:

(title ?? "").slice(0, 80)
