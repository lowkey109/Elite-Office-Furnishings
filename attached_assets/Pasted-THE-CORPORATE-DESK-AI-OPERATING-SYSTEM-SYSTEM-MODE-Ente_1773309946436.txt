ARCHITECTURAL REASONING

Layouts must reflect commercial architectural thinking.

Typical workstation standards:

Desk width
1200–1600mm

Desk depth
700–800mm

Circulation behind desks
900–1200mm

Meeting rooms should be placed along circulation routes but away from quiet work zones.
