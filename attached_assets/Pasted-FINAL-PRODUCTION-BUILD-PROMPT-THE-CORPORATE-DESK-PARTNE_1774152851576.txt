==================================================
REFERRAL MODEL TO IMPLEMENT
==================================================

This is NOT an affiliate coupon-link program.

This is a structured introducer / referral partner model for:
- commercial real estate agents
- tenant reps
- brokers
- architects
- fitout contacts
- project-side introducers
- workplace consultants
- aligned commercial partners

COMMISSION MODEL:
- flat 7.5% commission across approved, paid deals
- payout only after invoice/payment milestone or equivalent verified paid stage
- commission logic must be configurable in admin settings, defaulting to 7.5%

REFERRAL FLOW:
1. Partner lands on /partners
2. Partner applies or submits a live opportunity
3. Backend stores the application/referral
4. AI enriches and scores the referral
5. Referral is assigned / reviewed internally
6. Team qualifies the lead
7. Quote / layout / proposal is linked
8. Referral progresses through pipeline
9. Commission is calculated automatically when deal is marked won and paid
10. Partner sees status updates in portal
11. Admin/finance marks payout complete

==================================================
AI / INTELLIGENCE LOOP TO IMPLEMENT
==================================================

Loop the referral engine into the existing AI system and/or intelligence infrastructure.

Every referral submission must trigger AI enrichment.

AI MUST DO THESE 5 THINGS:
1. Summarize the referral into a concise executive brief
2. Score fit, urgency, expected value, and close likelihood
3. Classify the opportunity by segment and service line
4. Recommend next-best action for sales/admin
5. Cross-link referral data against existing intelligence systems where relevant:
   - office move radar
   - deal hunter
   - relocation signals
   - company intelligence
   - prior CRM/lead records
   - visitor/company activity if available

TARGET AI OUTPUT FIELDS:
- ai_summary
- ai_fit_score
- ai_urgency_score
- ai_close_likelihood_score
- ai_priority
- ai_recommended_owner
- ai_next_best_action
- ai_tags_json
- ai_risk_flags_json

AI TRIGGER POINTS:
- on partner application submitted
- on referral submitted
- when files/floor plans are uploaded
- when quote value changes
- when referral is stale for 3+ days
- when a deal is marked won or lost
- optionally on admin manual re-score

AI REQUIREMENTS:
- Use real model calls or real existing inference pipeline
- No fake generated scores hardcoded into UI
- If model fails, store a graceful fallback state and surface retry ability
- Log AI run metadata
- Make AI outputs visible in admin and partner workflows where appropriate
- Make referral scoring useful for triage, not just decorative
