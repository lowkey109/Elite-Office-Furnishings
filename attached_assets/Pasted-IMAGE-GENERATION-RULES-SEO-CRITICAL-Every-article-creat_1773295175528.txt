IMAGE GENERATION RULES (SEO CRITICAL)

Every article created for The Corporate Desk must include images.

Images improve:
• SEO rankings
• user engagement
• AI discovery systems
• page quality signals

For every article created:

Generate at least 3 images.

Required image types:

1️⃣ Hero Image (Top of Article)
A large premium image placed directly under the title.

2️⃣ Supporting Image 1 (Middle Section)
Used within a key section explaining layout planning or productivity.

3️⃣ Supporting Image 2 (Later Section)
Used in design trends or furniture discussion.

Image style must be:

• luxury commercial office interiors
• premium architecture
• modern executive desks
• natural lighting
• Australian CBD office environment
• ultra realistic
• editorial quality

Do NOT use:

• generic stock photos
• unrealistic AI scenes
• cartoon style images
• low resolution images

⸻

IMAGE IMPLEMENTATION

Images must be integrated into the article layout properly:

Hero image
→ below H1 title

Supporting images
→ inserted between major sections

Images must include:

• descriptive alt text
• descriptive filenames
• SEO-friendly captions where appropriate

Example alt text:

“modern executive office layout design with premium desks and natural lighting”

Example filename:

office-fitout-planning-modern-workspace.jpg

⸻

IMAGE PROMPT GENERATION

When creating images, use prompts similar to:

luxury corporate office interior, modern executive desks, natural lighting, glass meeting rooms, Australian CBD skyline, premium architectural photography, ultra realistic, 8k

modern collaborative workspace design, workstation clusters, glass meeting rooms, indoor plants, premium commercial office design, architectural photography

executive office interior design, luxury executive desk, leather chair, skyline view, sophisticated lighting, ultra realistic

⸻

IMAGE QUALITY CONTROL

Before publishing any article ensure:

• the hero image loads correctly
• images are high resolution
• images appear professional on desktop and mobile
• images visually match the article topic

⸻

IMPORTANT

If the CMS currently has no image system, implement one safely before publishing articles.

Do not publish articles without images.

⸻

That will make the AI add pictures automatically to every article.
