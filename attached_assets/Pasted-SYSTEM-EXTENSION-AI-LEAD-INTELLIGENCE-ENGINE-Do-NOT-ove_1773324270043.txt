SYSTEM EXTENSION — AI LEAD INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

Do NOT overwrite existing systems.

This upgrade adds an automated lead discovery and outreach system for The Corporate Desk.

The goal is to automatically identify companies likely to require office furniture, workspace planning, or office fit-outs.

------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------

The AI must continuously scan public sources for companies that may need:

• office furniture
• workspace planning
• office relocations
• office expansions
• new office leases

The AI should generate a list of qualified leads and automatically prepare outreach emails.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — LEAD DISCOVERY SOURCES
------------------------------------------------

The AI should scan publicly available sources for signals that a company may need office furniture.

Examples include:

LinkedIn company posts
LinkedIn hiring announcements
Commercial real estate listings
Startup funding announcements
Office relocation announcements
Coworking expansions
Business news sites
Job boards advertising large hiring campaigns

Lead signals include:

• "moving office"
• "new office"
• "expanding team"
• "opening new headquarters"
• "new lease signed"
• "office renovation"
• "hiring 20+ employees"

These signals indicate potential furniture demand.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — LEAD QUALIFICATION
------------------------------------------------

The AI must prioritize leads based on likelihood of purchasing office furniture.

Lead scoring factors:

company size
recent hiring activity
office relocation signals
industry type
location proximity
team growth rate

Example high-value lead:

tech startup
recent funding
expanding team
new office lease

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — CONTACT DISCOVERY
------------------------------------------------

For qualified leads the AI should attempt to identify decision makers.

Preferred contacts:

operations manager
office manager
facilities manager
founder
CEO
HR manager

Contact sources may include:

LinkedIn profiles
company websites
business directories

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 — HUMAN-STYLE EMAIL GENERATION
------------------------------------------------

The AI must generate outreach emails that feel natural and personal.

The emails must NOT sound automated.

Tone should be:

friendly
professional
brief
helpful

Avoid marketing language.

Example tone:

"Hi Sarah,

I noticed your team is expanding and it looks like you're growing quickly.

We work with companies planning new offices or upgrading their workspace and often help with layout planning and furniture sourcing.

If you're currently planning a workspace change I'd be happy to share a few ideas.

Best,
Ben"

Emails should feel like they were written by a real person.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 — EMAIL PERSONALIZATION
------------------------------------------------

Every outreach email should reference a real signal.

Examples:

recent hiring
new office
company expansion
team growth

This makes the email feel genuine rather than generic.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 — OUTREACH SCHEDULING
------------------------------------------------

Emails should be sent gradually to avoid spam signals.

Example:

10–20 emails per day.

Leads should be tracked in a lead database.

Fields:

company
contact_name
email
industry
location
lead_signal
date_contacted
response_status

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7 — RESPONSE MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------------------

When a lead replies the AI should:

notify admin
store conversation
suggest next steps

Example next steps:

offer free office layout plan
schedule consultation
request office floor plan

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8 — SALES FUNNEL CONNECTION
------------------------------------------------

When a lead engages, the AI should route them into the existing system:

workspace planner
quote builder
finance options
product catalog

This converts cold leads into active projects.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 9 — SAFETY
------------------------------------------------

Do not spam.
Respect rate limits.
Do not send duplicate emails.
Do not contact the same company repeatedly.

------------------------------------------------
RESULT
------------------------------------------------

After implementation the system should:

discover companies planning offices
identify decision makers
generate human-style outreach emails
send emails gradually
route replies into the workspace planning funnel

This system should continuously feed new leads into The Corporate Desk pipeline.
