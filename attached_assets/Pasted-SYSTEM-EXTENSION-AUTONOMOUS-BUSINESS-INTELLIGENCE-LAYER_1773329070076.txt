SYSTEM EXTENSION — AUTONOMOUS BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE LAYER

CRITICAL RULES

Do NOT overwrite existing systems.
Do NOT break:

• workspace planner
• estimator
• product catalog
• finance system
• Stripe payments
• admin dashboard
• lead intelligence
• procurement system
• supplier messaging
• email system

This extension adds a scheduled AI intelligence layer that continuously improves the business without manual intervention.

------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------

Create a background automation system that runs on scheduled intervals and continuously improves:

• market intelligence
• lead generation
• website performance
• conversion optimisation
• content growth
• system health
• supplier intelligence
• pricing awareness

The system must operate safely and autonomously.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — SCHEDULED JOB ENGINE
------------------------------------------------

Create a scheduled job system.

Jobs must run automatically at defined intervals.

Example schedules:

Every 6 hours
• scan for new office move signals
• scan for company growth signals

Every 12 hours
• scan commercial real estate news
• update building tenant intelligence

Every 24 hours
• analyze spending trends in office furniture
• update pricing intelligence
• check supplier pricing signals

Every 48 hours
• analyze website analytics
• detect conversion drop issues
• detect UX problems

Every 7 days
• generate SEO content
• publish new blog articles
• expand industry intelligence
• update trend reports

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — SPENDING TREND INTELLIGENCE
------------------------------------------------

The AI must monitor spending trends in commercial office furniture and workplace design.

Track signals such as:

• industry growth trends
• office fit-out spending trends
• workspace design trends
• hybrid work adoption
• office downsizing or expansion trends
• popular furniture categories
• price changes across suppliers

Sources may include:

industry reports
business news
commercial real estate data
workplace design publications
commercial furniture suppliers

The system must produce weekly insights such as:

• growing furniture categories
• declining categories
• pricing changes
• demand signals
• design trends

Store results inside admin intelligence reports.

------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — AUTOMATED SEO CONTENT ENGINE
------------------------------------------------

Create a scheduled SEO publishing system.

Every week the AI should generate high-quality articles about topics such as:

• office fit-out planning
• modern office design
• workplace productivity
• office layout strategy
• workspace psychology
• executive office design
• commercial furniture trends
