OBJECTIVE

Create a National Office Market Map that visualizes detected office activity across Australia.

This map will display companies likely to:

• relocate offices
• expand teams
• sign new leases
• open headquarters
• refurbish workspace

The map becomes the visual intelligence layer of the Office Move Radar.

--------------------------------------------------

MAP DATA SOURCES

Use data generated by:

• Office Move Radar
• hiring signal detection
• funding announcements
• news expansion signals
• commercial property listings
• pipeline opportunities

--------------------------------------------------

MAP DATA MODEL

Each map marker must include:

company_name
city
latitude
longitude
signal_type
estimated_headcount
estimated_office_size
estimated_project_value
confidence_score
timeline_estimate
source_url

SIGNAL TYPES

office_relocation
office_expansion
new_lease

workspace_refurbishment

/market-map

• interactive map

• filter by signal type
• filter by industry
• filter by project value
• filter by timeline
