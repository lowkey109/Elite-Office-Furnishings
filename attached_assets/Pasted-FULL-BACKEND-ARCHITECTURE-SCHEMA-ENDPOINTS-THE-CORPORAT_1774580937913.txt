==================================================
CRITICAL NON-NEGOTIABLES
==================================================

1. NO SYNTHETIC PRODUCTION DATA
Do not use fake/simulated company intelligence, fake office move signals, fake leads, fake pipeline records, or demo-generated revenue data in production paths.

2. REPLACE FRAGILE IN-PROCESS SCHEDULING
Current timer-based in-process scheduling is not acceptable.
Do NOT rely on setTimeout / setInterval for production jobs.
Move all recurring and delayed work to a persistent job system with locking, retries, idempotency, and audit logs.

3. DEDUPLICATION IS MANDATORY
The system must prevent duplicate:
- leads
- emails
- partner referrals
- office move signals
- company intelligence records
- follow-up sequences
- outbound actions

4. SECURITY IS MANDATORY
Client-side admin auth is not acceptable.
Replace weak sessionStorage-only admin gating with proper server-side authentication, authorization, session handling, and route protection.

5. EVERY ACTION MUST BE VERIFIED
Before sending, updating, creating, or pushing anything:
- validate input
- check dedupe rules
- verify record state
- log action
- enforce permissions

6. HUMAN REVIEW MODE MUST EXIST
For high-risk actions, support approval mode.
Examples:
- sending bulk outreach
- changing key pricing settings
- modifying partner payouts
- auto-pushing high-value deals to live customer workflows

7. ALL MODULES MUST LOOP TOGETHER
This is one operating system, not disconnected features.
