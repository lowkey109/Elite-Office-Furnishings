CONVERSATION FLOW

For each WhatsApp message:

1 capture sender phone number
2 capture message text
3 retrieve conversation history
4 combine history and message
5 send to AI model
6 generate contextual reply
7 return reply as Twilio TwiML
8 store updated conversation history

--------------------------------------------------

CONVERSATION MEMORY

Store conversation history by phone number.

Structure:

phone_number
conversation_history

Keep the most recent 20 messages.

This allows the AI to remember context and avoid repeating itself.

SMART SALES BEHAVIOUR

The AI should guide conversations intelligently.
