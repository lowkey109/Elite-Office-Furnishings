PHASE 3 — LEAD DETECTION RULES
==================================================

The AI should identify leads when one or more of these signals appear:

HIGH INTENT SIGNALS
- company relocating office
- tenant lease expiry approaching
- office fit-out / refurbishment mentioned
- new office opening
- facilities/workplace hiring for a known office location

MEDIUM INTENT SIGNALS
- strong headcount growth
- multiple office admin/workplace roles posted
- new city expansion
- office demand / tenant movement in target building or precinct

LOWER INTENT SIGNALS
- company posts about workplace culture / new workspace
- general hiring growth without workplace-specific roles
