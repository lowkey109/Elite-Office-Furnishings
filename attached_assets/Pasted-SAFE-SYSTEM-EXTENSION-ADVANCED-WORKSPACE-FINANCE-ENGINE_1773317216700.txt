==================================================

Upgrade the site so monthly repayments can be shown automatically for:

1. AI planner results
2. advanced estimator results
3. finance page
4. relevant product/range pages
5. quote-related outputs where appropriate

The goal is to turn large upfront project values into easy monthly payment framing.

Examples:
- $25,000 workspace → estimated monthly payment
- $58,000 workspace → estimated monthly payment
- $120,000 office fit-out → estimated monthly payment

==================================================
PART 3 — CALCULATION LOGIC
==================================================

Build an indicative repayment engine.

Required inputs:
- estimated project value
- finance term
- indicative interest factor or finance multiplier
- furniture only vs full fit-out

Default term options:
- 24 months
- 36 months
- 48 months
- 60 months

If no lender-specific rate is supplied, use clearly labelled indicative finance assumptions only.

The output must be presented as:
- indicative monthly repayment
- selected term
- note that final approval and pricing are subject to lender assessment

Do NOT present fake guaranteed finance approvals.
Do NOT present exact lender-issued offers unless real lender data exists.
