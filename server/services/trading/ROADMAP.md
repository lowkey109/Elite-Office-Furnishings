Build the platform as a unified autonomous revenue intelligence engine.

The system must support:
1. wallet identity scoring
2. wallet action ledgering
3. capital flow aggregation
4. copyability scoring
5. allocator-based capital distribution
6. risk governor veto logic
7. reasoning graph generation
8. trade explanation generation
9. outcome learning updates
10. scenario simulation and stress testing
11. cross-domain signal fusion
12. opportunity-level orchestration across trading and business actions

The system must remain modular.
The system must not fake data.
The system must not present mock data as live.
All actions must be typed, logged, replayable, explainable, and risk-constrained.
The risk governor must be able to block any trade regardless of strategy confidence.
Wallet skill must be based on consistency and survivability, not raw PnL alone.
Learning must update scores only from actual outcomes, not assumptions.
